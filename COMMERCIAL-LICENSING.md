# Commercial Licensing

CloudDialect follows a dual-licensing model:

1. **Community License**: qualifying Community Use under [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
2. **Paid Commercial Agreement**: required for use in or for any Revenue-Generating Product or Service, including intended monetization before the product receives its first payment.

## Commercial use requiring permission

Commercial permission is needed when the Framework is materially used to build, host, distribute, serve or operate a monetized offering, including:

- Paid SaaS, mobile applications, packaged applications, paid APIs and licensed software products.
- A free tier that is part of a paid/freemium offering, or an advertising-supported product.
- An agency or consultant delivering a client product that uses the Framework when the client product or delivered service is commercial.
- OEM embedding, resale, white-labeling or hosted resale of the Framework.
- Products planned for commercial launch, even before their first sale.

A company's unrelated revenue does not alone transform genuinely separate noncommercial learning or evaluation into Commercial Use. Borderline cases should be agreed in writing; interpretive examples are not a substitute for the license.

## How to obtain permission

Start by opening a [public licensing inquiry](https://github.com/VincentChuWaiChow/CloudDialect/issues/new). Share **only non-confidential basic information** there. After initial contact, agree a separate private channel for the following contracting details:

- Legal company name and non-confidential product/service description.
- Whether the use is SaaS, on-prem distribution, mobile, API, OEM or consulting.
- Customer/tenant scope and proposed deployment/redistribution model.
- Expected annual product revenue and desired fee model, if available.
- Intended launch date and required support or warranty terms.

No commercial rights are granted until **both parties sign a commercial license** and the contractual payment conditions are satisfied.

## Fees

### Standard nonexclusive resale proposal

The owner-selected standard proposal for new **nonexclusive resale** agreements
is a **10% royalty on Covered Receipts**, reported and paid monthly, within
**30 calendar days after month-end**.

**Covered Receipts** means payments actually collected from customers for the
CloudDialect resale offerings expressly named in the signed agreement, less
only actual refunds of those payments and separately collected VAT/sales taxes
payable to the tax authority. This is a revenue base, not profit, and excludes
unrelated company revenue. Salaries, marketing, overhead and other operating
costs are not royalty deductions.

- Agree allocation rules for bundles and collection through affiliates before
  signing; the CloudDialect portion must not simply be assigned zero.
- Complete the currency, exchange-rate method, refund adjustments, monthly
  statement contents, records retention and proportionate audit process in the
  signed agreement. No minimum or cap is invented by this proposal.
- Price any hosting, support or maintenance obligations separately; the royalty
  does not promise unlimited services.

For example, 10,000 in Covered Receipts produces a royalty of 1,000 in the agreed
currency. It is not a share of company ownership or unrelated future revenue.

Other commercial uses remain subject to separately negotiated terms; this
proposal must not be treated as a blanket 10% rate or a change to the Community
License's commercial-use boundary. Any alternative resale arrangement requires
the Licensor's express agreement in the signed agreement.

This informational proposal does not impose a payment obligation or grant
commercial rights by itself. Complete the agreement and obtain qualified legal
review before execution. Existing agreements are not amended by this proposal;
any change to existing agreements requires its own agreed amendment.

Exact placement, formats, brand use and any restrictions are set out in the signed agreement. See [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).
