## Project status

**Foundation stage.** This repository starter contains CloudDialect branding, a proposed community-use license, commercial-licensing materials, and contribution guidance. It **does not yet contain a working framework implementation**. No claim of production readiness is made.

**Legal status:** The text in `LICENSE.md` is a **draft for legal review, not an operative license grant**. The copyright owner, contact address, governing law and contributor intake process must be finalized before accepting contributions or releasing licensed framework code. Until then, **all rights remain reserved** to the extent permitted by law. Do not treat this repository as MIT, Apache, GPL or other OSI-approved open source.

## Proposed dual-licensing policy

| Scenario | Intended permission once license is finalized |
|---|---|
| Personal study, independent research and noncommercial experimentation | Community license (free) |
| Noncommercial projects and community contributions | Community license, with conditions |
| Isolated internal evaluation by a company | Community license (free) |
| Paid SaaS, subscription products, paid applications and paid APIs using CloudDialect | **Paid commercial agreement required before commercial use** |
| Freemium, advertisement-funded or planned revenue-generating products | **Paid commercial agreement required before commercial use** |
| OEM resale, white-label distribution or commercialization of CloudDialect itself | **Paid commercial agreement and attribution required** |

**Payment is not replaced by attribution.** Commercial users will need an executed agreement defining scope, fees and required credit. If the project owner subsequently adopts the proposed policy, any revenue-generating product that uses CloudDialect in a covered manner will require commercial authorization before launch.
